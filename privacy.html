Privacy Policy

ClipCourt

Your videos are processed on your device. We do not upload them for analytics.

About This Policy

ClipCourt is a video editing app provided by Kostas Papadopoulos. This policy describes how ClipCourt handles information when you use the app, visit this website, or contact us.

Current status, October 20, 2026: Custom usage statistics are not enabled in the currently distributed app. We are preparing the limited statistics feature described below. It will remain disabled until the service and applicable regional requirements have been reviewed. This policy does not itself enable collection.

Your Videos and Projects

Editing and exporting take place on your device. We do not upload your videos, audio, thumbnails, filenames, folder paths, annotations, team names, scores, or project contents to our analytics service. Projects and preferences are stored locally. Media you select or save may also be managed by Apple Photos, iCloud, or a storage provider you choose, according to your own settings.

ClipCourt uses photo-library and file access to import selected videos and save exports. You control these permissions through your device settings. Choosing to share an export sends it only through the destination you select.

Limited Usage Statistics in Future Versions

Where enabled and permitted, ClipCourt will send a small set of measurements to help us understand usage and improve export reliability:

  • Export starts, completed exports, failures, cancellations, and the number of files successfully saved, including partial exports.
  • Selected export options: quick or full export, combined or separate files, resolution and frame-rate categories, quality category, processing mode, scoreboard enabled or disabled, and saving to Photos or a folder.
  • Export duration ranges and broad failure categories, such as encoding or saving. We do not send raw error messages or crash logs to this service.
  • App version, iOS or Mac platform, and a daily activity measurement indicating a first observed or returning day.

These measurements do not include names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, installation identifiers, precise location, or media contents. We do not create individual usage histories, use analytics for advertising, sell analytics data, or combine it with information from other apps or websites.

In the proposed design, a daily marker stays on your device to limit activity reporting to one attempt per day and distinguish a first observed day from a returning day. The marker expires after a fixed 90-day window that is not extended by visits. It is not a unique identifier and is not transmitted. The service stores separate daily totals and category counts, rather than individual event records or a history for an installation.

Regional Availability

Custom usage statistics remain disabled in every region. The current app has no analytics setting because it does not send these measurements or contact the eligibility service. We will update this policy with the final regional scope, legal basis, and applicable rights before introducing collection.

We plan to enable statistics only in regions where collection without an opt-in is permitted and applicable platform requirements are satisfied. Where consent is required, or eligibility cannot be established, custom statistics will remain off. Continuing to use the app is not treated as consent.

If this feature is introduced, the app first contacts the service to check regional eligibility without including export or activity details. Cloudflare, our planned hosting provider, processes the connection's IP address and supplies an approximate country and region for this check. We do not use GPS or retain the IP address or region in the analytics database. Network-based location is approximate and can be affected by VPNs and travel.

Service Providers and Network Information

We plan to use Cloudflare Workers and D1 to receive measurements and store aggregate statistics. Network delivery necessarily exposes connection information, including IP addresses, to the hosting provider. Provider security and operational processing is separate from our aggregate analytics database; this is not a promise that no personal information is ever processed in transit. Before activation, we will review the provider's data-processing terms, logging settings, retention, and any international transfers and update this policy with applicable details.

This website is hosted by Vercel. Visiting it involves ordinary web requests, including an IP address and browser information, which Vercel processes to deliver and protect the site. The website does not add analytics scripts or advertising trackers. See Vercel's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Apple may provide App Store usage and diagnostic reports under Apple's own settings and policies. Those reports are separate from the custom statistics described here. If purchases are offered, Apple handles payment information through the App Store; we do not receive payment-card details.

Retention and Deletion

The planned analytics service retains daily aggregate counters for a rolling 90 days, with automated daily deletion of older counters. Hosting-provider backup retention is separate and will be documented before activation. Measurements awaiting delivery are held briefly in app memory, are not saved as an offline history, and are discarded if delivery fails.

Because the aggregate counters contain no installation identifier, we cannot retrieve or remove an individual installation's contribution from those totals. Custom usage statistics are currently disabled, so there are no contributions to stop. Your local projects and preferences remain under your control; removing the app does not delete exports saved separately to Photos or another folder.

Contact and Privacy Requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your request. Please avoid sending videos or sensitive information unless necessary for your request. You can ask us to delete support correspondence when it is no longer needed, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact clipcourt.app@gmail.com for questions or requests. We do not collect additional identifying information solely to identify a contribution to aggregate statistics.

Children

ClipCourt does not require an account or ask for a child's name or contact details. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and provide additional notice where required.
