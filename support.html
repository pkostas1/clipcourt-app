Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use ClipCourt?

Open a video from your Camera Roll, watch it play, and tap "Tap to Clip" whenever something good is happening. Tap again to stop clipping. When you're done, tap Export to save your highlight reel.

What video formats are supported?

ClipCourt supports all video formats that iOS can play, including .mp4, .mov, and .m4v.

Will my progress be saved if I close the app?

Yes. ClipCourt automatically saves your editing session. When you reopen the app, you'll pick up right where you left off.

What export options are available?

You can export as a single highlight reel or as separate clips. You can choose resolution (up to 4K) and frame rate (up to 60fps). Lossless export preserves original quality; re-encoded export lets you downscale to save space.

Does ClipCourt work on iPad?

Yes. ClipCourt works on both iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

How do I add a scoreboard?

Tap the Settings icon, then tap Scoreboard. Enter your team names and tap Save. After each clip, you can assign a point to either team.

How do I delete a clip?

Tap the three-dot menu on any green clip in the timeline, then tap "Delete Clip."

Can I share my highlight reel?

Yes. After exporting, tap "Share Video" to send it via iMessage, AirDrop, Instagram, or any other sharing method.
